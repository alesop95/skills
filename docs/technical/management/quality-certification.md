# Quality Certification

## Overview

Support to formal quality and compliance frameworks affecting IT
operations, including ISO 9000 procedures, business continuity, and
disaster recovery documentation. Includes hands-on development of custom
queries against business systems to extract measurable indicators.

## Technologies & tools

- ISO 9000 procedural frameworks for infrastructure management
- Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
  templates
- Italian Legislative Decree 51/2018 — procedural documentation support
  (breach response itself is owned by Cybersecurity & IT Governance)
- Custom query layers on business management software

## Responsibilities & operational scope

- Quality certification support
- Drafting assistance on:
  - Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)
  - Data breach register and notification to the supervisory authority
    (GDPR + Legislative Decree 51/2018) — documentation support only, see
    Cybersecurity & IT Governance for the response process
  - Infrastructure management procedures (ISO 9000)
  - Regulations on the use of computer systems
- Implementation of custom queries on business management software for
  information extraction (customer turnover distribution, orders,
  commercial data)
- Conceptual breakdown of directly measurable business processes
