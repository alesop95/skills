# ERP & Business Applications

## Overview

Maintenance, development, and major-version migration of ERP systems
supporting corporate operations, including technical project management
of cross-version module migrations. Covers collaboration with external
software houses and direct intervention on business processes via the
ERP platform.

## Technologies & tools

- **Odoo** — primary ERP platform, with hands-on experience across
  versions 12 through 18
- Module-level customization within the Odoo framework
- See also IT Administration & Billing for ERP-integrated invoicing

## Responsibilities & operational scope

- Maintenance of ERP systems to ensure operational efficiency
- Analysis of business processes and collaboration with external software
  houses
- ERP development (see also Ad-hoc Internal Development and Full-Stack
  Development for the underlying programming skills)
- Technical project management of ERP module migration between major
  versions (Odoo v12 → v18)
