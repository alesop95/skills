## Responsibilities & operational scope

### Leadership and decision-making

**Teamwork oriented, technical leadership, decision-making.** Coordinates the IT team at Intrawelt as IT Manager, holding budget ownership and roadmap decisions. Served as regional councilor for ADMO (the Italian bone-marrow donor association) for three years, a volunteer leadership role involving event organization and coordination with other regional groups. Cross-team coordination also shows up earlier in the career: as Product Manager at Clementoni, working across Operations, Marketing and Sales to bring new toy products from concept to launch, with direct P&L exposure through the cost analysis behind each product decision.

### Communication and negotiation

**Good communicator, good listener, negotiation.** Reported directly to stakeholders and the CEO on product strategy and cost analysis at Clementoni, and negotiated with that company's foreign suppliers directly. Negotiated again, in a different register, with international clients and overseas production teams as an electroacoustic product engineer at Elettromedia, translating technical requirements into design options across a language and cultural gap. Two different products, two different counterparts, same underlying skill exercised twice. Actively studying listening and communication in a more structured way through an EFT (Emotionally Focused Therapy) couples-therapy course and a public-speaking and diction course, both applying directly to how feedback and disagreement get handled at work.

### Analytical and strategic thinking

**Critical thinking, strategic vision, eye for detail.** Defines the IT roadmap and governance framework at Intrawelt, weighing build-vs-buy and vendor tradeoffs. Financial KPI analysis and business strategy were the core of a part-time Master in Business Strategy and Management (ISTAO). Attention to detail is a working requirement, not a preference, in two very different contexts: electroacoustic tuning and quality assessment of complex audio systems at Elettromedia, and being the internal lead for ISO/IEC 27001 compliance, where a missed control is a real audit finding. Product decisions at Clementoni combined market and competitor analysis with a pedagogical lens (UX designed around how children actually learn through play), tracked end-to-end via PLM rather than left as an intuition call.

### Adaptability and resilience

**Flexibility, resilience, adaptability, emotional intelligence.** The career itself is a case study in adaptability: from electroacoustic product engineering, to product management for a toy manufacturer, to IT management, each transition required picking up a materially different skill set rather than deepening the same one. Emotional intelligence is something actively studied, not just claimed, through psychology-adjacent coursework (EFT couples therapy) and through volunteering as a catechism educator, which requires reading a room of teenagers and adjusting on the fly.

### Organization and continuous learning

**Time management, problem-solving, continuous learning, proactive attitude.** Holds internal responsibility for ISO/IEC 27001 and is preparing the CompTIA Security+ exam alongside a full IT Manager workload, which only works with deliberate time management. Problem-solving shows up concretely in moving a company's infrastructure and knowledge base to a self-hosted, on-premise setup (RAG system, Qdrant, Ollama, n8n) rather than defaulting to a SaaS subscription, and, earlier, in keeping loudspeaker programs for international clients on track at Elettromedia by managing production-flow issues with overseas manufacturing teams directly instead of escalating and waiting. The pattern across the whole career, technical and non-technical alike, is picking up what a project needs next instead of waiting to be asked.
