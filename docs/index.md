## Role overview

Designs, implements, and manages the technical and organizational measures required to protect informational assets, including security procedures, incident management, and IT-related business continuity.

Coordinates the IT team and plays a strategic role in the definition and implementation of the corporate technology roadmap. Holds full responsibility for the IT infrastructure and software development activities, with particular focus on SaaS solutions, ensuring adoption of modern cybersecurity best practices.

Acts as system administrator and security lead for the entire IT infrastructure, including the design and operation of structured networks (LAN, WAN, VPN, VLAN, firewalls), configuration and maintenance of physical and virtual servers, and implementation of backup and disaster-recovery policies.

Ensures compliance with ISO/IEC 27001 and GDPR, and oversees the adoption of protection tooling (EDR, anti-malware, endpoint security).

Holds responsibility for IT governance, vendor management, and integration of ERP and business applications, contributing to the resilience and efficiency of corporate processes.

Supports executive direction in defining IT strategy and digital transformation, with cross-cutting competence in networking, software development, AI, virtualization, and cloud. Actively contributes to the creation and maintenance of structured technical documentation, recognized as a strategic industrial asset capturing the state of the art, the implemented configurations, and the adopted/planned technical decisions.
